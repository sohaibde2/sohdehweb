FAQ

Common questions

What is DzTracking?

A free Android app that tracks Algérie Poste, EMS, Cainiao and other international parcels in one dashboard, and surfaces live AliExpress deals and discount codes.

Which carriers are supported?

Algérie Poste, EMS, Cainiao, UPU, Emirates Post, Malaysia Post and other major networks used for AliExpress, Temu and Shein shipments.

Is it free?

Yes — DzTracking is free to download and use on Android.

How do I track an AliExpress order?

Paste the tracking number into the app and it follows the parcel automatically from the seller's warehouse to your door.

Does it share my tracking numbers?

No. Tracking numbers and parcel data stay local to your device and are never sold or shared.
