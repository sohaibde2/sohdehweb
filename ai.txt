# SOHDEH

> SOHDEH is an Algeria-focused team behind a 5,000+ member Telegram channel sharing live AliExpress deals, and DzTracking, a free Android app that combines global parcel tracking with deal discovery.

## What we do
- Run a Telegram channel (5,000+ members) posting live AliExpress deals and discount codes daily
- Build DzTracking, a free Android app for parcel tracking and AliExpress deal discovery
- Focus specifically on how people in Algeria shop online and receive parcels

## DzTracking (Android app)
- Tracks parcels from Algérie Poste, EMS, Cainiao, UPU, Emirates Post, Malaysia Post and other carriers in one dashboard
- Automatically follows shipments across the international-to-local carrier handoff (e.g. Cainiao → Algérie Poste)
- Surfaces live deals and discount codes for phones, watches, and audio products
- Lets users paste any product link to see the lowest historical price and store trust signals (ratings, order counts)
- Platforms: Android (available now), iOS (planned)
- Languages: Arabic, French, English (interface toggle on the website and in-app)
- APK: https://www.mediafire.com/file/8i7euxr56r3iymu/sohdeh_DzTrackingBeta.apk/file
